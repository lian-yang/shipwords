# Video credit pricing

This is the pricing basis for the local catalog, not a guarantee of realized net profit. Prices below are the median of Yir's published callable-channel prices for the same output specification, observed on 2026-11-06. Official comparison prices marked unavailable through Yir are excluded. When a specification has only one callable channel, its price is the median. Token-billed estimates exclude unlisted input charges. See the [Yir model catalog](https://yir.ai/models) and individual model price tables; prices and channel eligibility can change.

Retail credit tables are in `site.models.ts`; `src/video-pricing.server.ts` sets per-job upstream ceilings. The reference credit value is the lowest listed pack rate, $19.99 / 500 = $0.03998 per credit. For each published specification, the credit price rounds up median output cost divided by that value and the target cost share. Cost shares are 70%, 65%, 60%, or 50% of the assumed revenue; more expensive rates use lower cost shares. The upstream `max_cost` also uses no more than the job's recorded credits, so an old queued job cannot gain a higher cap when prices change. Discounts, historical promotional credits, fees, taxes, free trials and charged failures affect realized profit and are not included in this calculation.

For example, [Seedance 2.5](https://yir.ai/models/seedance-2-5) 1080p with no audio has four callable published rates: $0.5346, $0.6240, $0.7900 and $1.1372 per second. The median is $0.7070 per second. Thirty seconds cost $21.21 at that reference rate; targeting 50% output-cost margin at $0.03998 per credit requires 1,062 credits. Actual provider charges may differ; [Yir's max_cost](https://yir.ai/docs/concepts/billing) limits total billed attempts but a tight cap can make a request fail.

The free choices are Seedance 2.5 (480p / 4s), MiniMax H3 (480p / 4s), Seedance 2.0 Mini (480p / 4s), Kling 2.6 (720p / 5s), Grok Imagine Video (480p / 6s) and Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (360p / 3s). These are minimums from the model contracts. On 2026-11-06, authenticated, text-only quotes returned available supply for all six; the respective expected amounts were $0.38432, $0.0415, $0.072605, $0.184, $0.0612 and $0.069528. Gemini's output price was an estimate, and known input costs are included in the expected amount. The Seedance 2.5 1080p / 30s quote was $16.038. Quotes do not lock prices or establish availability for image inputs; no live generation was submitted.

The current tool accepts text and at most one image. Video/audio-input models are excluded by the product decision; specifications without sufficiently comparable published prices are also not represented. On 2026-11-06, the default text-only settings for all 27 text-capable catalog entries returned available supply and an expected amount within the corresponding paid cost cap. The three image-required models also returned supply and estimates within the paid caps when quoted using a public image URL; the six trial models did likewise for image input. A URL quote is not proof that a generation can fetch or process that image. Quotes are point-in-time estimates, and other inputs or later requests may have different supply or prices.
